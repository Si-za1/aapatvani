Architecture: one deployable, and where the model is allowed to stand

A single Next.js app on Cloud Run. The model is reached only from the server, and every backend behind it can be swapped by an environment variable.

Process flow: a report to an approved alert

Every stage names who does it, because the whole point of the product is that a model's output and a person's decision are never the same kind of thing.

Use cases, by who drives them

Three actors outside the system, and the work each of them can start. The dashed links are the ones a reviewer never triggers directly: the system does them on their behalf, and says so.

What the four colours mean, everywhere in the product

  • ReportedA community member said it. The words are in their text, which is not the same as true.
  • AI interpretationGemini read it, and the cited excerpt or media item is stored with it.
  • ComputedDeterministic code worked it out over a versioned dataset. The same inputs give the same answer.
  • ReviewerA named person decided it. This is the only source of the word Confirmed.
