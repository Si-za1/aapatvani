Wireframes

Eight screens, drawn as structure rather than as design: what is on each one, what a person does there, and which part carries the idea. Nothing here is decorated, because the point of a wireframe is to be argued with before it is built.

Grey blocks are placeholders; the words that appear are the ones that carry meaning. Every screen here is built and running in the prototype — these are the schematics of it, not plans for something else.
