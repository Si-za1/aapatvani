Free tier today · paid per report at scale

What AapatVani costs to run

The cost tracks reports, not users. Nothing runs between reports, so an idle deployment costs nothing and a district can leave it switched on.

At three scales

This demo ~50 reports a month $0 free tier
One district 500 reports a month ~$2 per month
National 10,000 reports a month ~$42 per month

Where the $42 goes, at 10,000 reports a month

99% Gemini, reading reports $41.64 · one extraction and one embedding each
1% Everything else $0.22 · Cloud Run, Firestore and Cloud Storage
✓ No fixed monthly cost ✓ Scales to zero when idle ✓ Paid per report, not per seat

Estimate. Assumes Gemini Flash list pricing, and one extraction, one embedding and one comparison per report; a measured extraction is 1,734 tokens in and 833 out. Cloud Run and Firestore stay inside their free tiers at every scale shown, so the infrastructure cost is Cloud Storage alone.
